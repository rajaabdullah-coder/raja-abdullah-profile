About

I build computer vision and Edge AI systems that run on small, affordable hardware, and put them to work in biometrics and smart agriculture.

I am a Senior Lecturer at the Faculty of Intelligent Computing, Universiti Malaysia Perlis. My PhD (2022) developed independent LBP feature fusion for face recognition. Since then my work has moved from the lab into farms, factories and rail: IoT fertigation and spraying systems, silage and mushroom monitoring, and embedded sub-systems for KTM trains. I welcome collaboration with industry and enquiries from prospective postgraduate students.
